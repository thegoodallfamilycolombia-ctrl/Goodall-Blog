Meet the Goodalls

We are the Goodall Family

We're Ryan & Claudia, along with our two boys, Levi and Neal — missionaries serving in Santa Marta, Colombia. We've been living here for about six months now, and we are just barely getting settled in.

This blog page is for those who may be interested in learning more about us. The plan is to upload videos with the kids from time to time — with some funny antics or little "bits of knowledge" — and to post our monthly prayer letters so you can always see what is going on. Every now and then, I may share a few personal thoughts or devotionals in blog form too.

The hope is that you get to know us better, and that we can be more transparent about what's really going on — through video, pictures, and honest writing. Of course, we'd love any feedback you may want to give us!

We are not trying to become missionary influencers. We simply want to give you a little inside view of what it's like to be a missionary family in another country. We hope you all enjoy!
